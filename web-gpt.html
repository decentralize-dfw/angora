01 / The residenceAn established address. A personal way to live.

The space to live.
The freedom to
make it yours.

On Hatırlı Sokak, in Ankara’s Angora Evleri, this detached villa brings together a private garden, a swimming pool and four distinct levels of living.

The slope of the site gives the home two different arrivals: a street-level entrance for everyday life, and a lower level that opens directly onto the terrace, garden and water.

Five bedrooms. Four living spaces. Three kitchens. Enough independence for different generations, with plenty of room to come together.

03 / Inside the homeWarmth. Character. Room to breathe.

Come together.
Feel at home.

This is a home with its own character: timber floors, generous windows, wrought-iron stair rails and rooms that connect through a central hall. The social spaces and private rooms have their own place in the house.

The entrance level

A room for
everyone.

The main living and dining room extends across approximately 53 m². Large windows bring in daylight, while the balcony looks towards the pool and garden below.

At street level, the entrance hall connects this social floor to the kitchen, staircase, lift and garage.

The first floor

A little more
privacy.

The principal suite brings together a bedroom, a separate dressing room and an en-suite bathroom. A balcony adds a quiet connection to the outdoors.

Two further bedrooms, a family bathroom and a sitting area share this level, keeping the family’s private rooms together.

For the
everyday rituals.

Three kitchens. Four living spaces.

The entrance-level kitchen has room for breakfast. The garden floor has its own kitchen for days by the pool. Upstairs, an attic kitchenette gives guests or grown children a little more independence.

01

A lift & a staircase

A lift serves the garden, entrance and first floors. The attic is reached by the staircase, whose iron balustrades and timber handrails carry the character of the home between levels.

02

Space for arriving

An internally connected garage and open parking provide room for three vehicles in total. The covered entrance creates a sheltered transition from the street into the home.

03

Independent possibilities

The annexe has its own entrance. Together with a kitchen on the lower floor and a kitchenette in the attic, the layout accommodates guests, family members or working from home.

04

Everyday essentials

Gas combi heating, a 6-tonne water tank, three bathrooms, two guest WCs and two balconies complement the living spaces and garden.

05 / The neighbourhoodAngora Evleri · Ankara
Mutlukent, Çankaya

A quieter
side of
the city.

Angora Evleri is an established villa neighbourhood in Ankara’s Mutlukent district. Detached homes, mature gardens and residential streets give the setting its own scale and rhythm.

The villa sits on Hatırlı Sokak, with the Eskişehir road and Bilkent connections placing it within reach of the wider city.
