01

A lift & a staircase

A lift serves the garden, entrance and first floors. The attic is reached by the staircase, whose iron balustrades and timber handrails carry the character of the home between levels.

02

Space for arriving

An internally connected garage and open parking provide room for three vehicles in total. The covered entrance creates a sheltered transition from the street into the home.

The annexe has its own entrance. Together with a kitchen on the lower floor and a kitchenette in the attic, the layout accommodates guests, family members or working from home.

06 / The neighbourhoodAngora Evleri · Ankara
Mutlukent, Çankaya

A quieter side of the city.

Angora Evleri is an established villa neighbourhood in Ankara’s Mutlukent district. Detached homes, mature gardens and residential streets give the setting its own scale and rhythm.

The villa sits on Hatırlı Sokak, with the Eskişehir road and Bilkent connections placing it within reach of the wider city.
